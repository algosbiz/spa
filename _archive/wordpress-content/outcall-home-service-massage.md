### Frequently Asked Questions

What treatments are available for home service?
All massage treatments listed on our spa menu are available for home service. If you’re unsure which treatment to choose, our team will be happy to assist.

Are home service prices the same as in-spa treatments?
Yes, home service prices are the same as our in-spa treatments, with an additional home service fee of IDR 75,000 per therapist.

Do we need to prepare anything before the therapist arrives?

No preparation is needed. We bring everything required for your session. A quiet and comfortable space is all we ask.

Does home service cover hotels and villas?
Yes, our home service massage is available for private homes, villas, and hotels in Seminyak and the surrounding areas.

Can I book on the same day?
Same-day bookings can be made with therapist availability.
