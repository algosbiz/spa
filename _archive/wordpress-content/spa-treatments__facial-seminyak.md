### Learn More about Spa Bali Moon's Face Peeling Masks

Read below to see the step-by-step process of our facial treatment, including how the face peeling mask is applied based on your skin’s needs.

### Gentle Cleansing to Prepare Your Skin

We begin with a light cleanse using a gentle milk cleanser and warm towels. This step removes makeup and surface buildup without irritating the skin. It helps refresh the face and prepare it for exfoliation. The skin is left clean, comfortable, and ready for the next step.

### Warm Steam to Open the Pores

After cleansing, warm steam is applied evenly across the face. The heat helps soften the skin and open the pores. This process loosens trapped impurities and buildup. It also prepares the skin to respond better to exfoliation.

### Red Clay Scrub for Gentle Exfoliation

Once the pores are open, a mild Red Clay scrub is applied. This step helps remove dead skin cells and remaining impurities. The exfoliation is gentle and controlled throughout the treatment. The skin feels smoother and more even after this step.

### Argan Oil Massage for Relaxation and Circulation

Pure Argan Oil is used for a lymphatic facial massage. This massage technique helps encourage the movement of lymph fluid and supports the removal of toxins. It also improves circulation and helps reduce puffiness in the face. The skin appears fresher and more relaxed afterward.

### Targeted Mask with Activator Gel

Next, we apply either a Tea Tree Mask or Gold Mask, depending on your skin’s needs. The mask is blended with our Activator Gel containing Aloe Vera, Pro-vitamin B5, Licorice, Cucumber, and Chamomile. Tea Tree helps balance oil and calm blemish-prone skin, while Gold supports hydration, brightness, and elasticity. Both options work to support skin comfort and overall condition.

### Rose Water Toning to Finish

To complete the facial, rose water is gently applied as a final step. This helps soothe the skin and restore moisture balance. It also refines the appearance of pores after the treatment. The skin feels calm, refreshed, and settled at the end of the facial.

### How We Guide Your Facial Treatment

Here’s what you can expect during your facial with us, where each step is handled with care

Step 1: Cleansing
We begin with a gentle milk cleanser that softly removes impurities and surface buildup. This first touch clears the way, preparing your skin to fully benefit from what comes next.

Step 2: Steaming
Warm steam follows, opening the pores and allowing the skin to soften. This gentle heat enhances nutrient absorption and encourages a naturally healthy glow.

Step 3: Gentle Scrub
After steaming, a mild red clay scrub is applied to exfoliate gently. It helps remove dead skin cells and impurities while leaving the skin soft, smooth, and ready for the next step.

Step 4: Massage
Using pure Argan Oil, we deliver a gentle massage that relaxes the muscles, boosts circulation, and nourishes the skin. This step is both calming and revitalizing.

Step 5: Mask Application
Next comes the Tea Tree Mask, blended with our Activator Gel. This step helps balance oil, soothe redness, and reduce blemishes, leaving the skin soft, fresh, and clear.

Step 6: Toning
To finish, rose water is applied to tone and refresh. This final touch refines the pores, restores comfort, and leaves your skin balanced, hydrated, and ready to face the day.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Which skin types are suitable for this facial?
The facial can be used on all skin types. Tea Tree mask applies for oily or blemish-prone skin, and Gold mask is great for ensuring hydration, firmness, and radiance.

How long does each session take?
In a full facial session, it takes 60 to 75 minutes for cleansing, steaming, Argan Oil massage, and mask application.

Can I choose between the Tea Tree and Gold masks?
Yes. Clients may select Tea Tree for calming and balancing oil production, or Gold for deep hydration, improved elasticity, and a revitalized appearance.

How frequently should the facial be performed?
For optimal results, it is recommended every 3 to 4 weeks. Regular treatments help maintain hydration, clarity, and skin texture.

Is this facial suitable for sensitive skin?
Yes. The treatment incorporates soothing ingredients such as Aloe Vera, Chamomile, and Cucumber in the Activator Gel. Our therapists can modify the procedure to accommodate any skin sensitivities.
