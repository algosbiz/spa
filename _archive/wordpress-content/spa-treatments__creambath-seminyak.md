### Choose the Right Cream Bath for Your Hair at Spa Bali Moon

Explore the benefits of our specially chosen ingredients and pick the perfect one to give your hair the care it deserves during your next Cream Bath treatment

### Ginseng

Ginseng stimulates hair follicles and supports growth, helping reduce hair loss and improve thickness. Its anti-inflammatory properties promote a healthier scalp, while strengthening hair strands to reduce breakage and split ends. Improved scalp circulation enhances nutrient delivery, helping maintain stronger, healthier hair over time.

### Avocado

Avocado is rich in natural oils that deeply condition and hydrate hair, while vitamins A, D, E, and B-complex strengthen strands and nourish the scalp. Its properties fortify the hair shaft, reduce breakage, and improve resilience, leaving hair soft, smooth, shiny, and healthy, while supporting overall scalp health.

### Aloe Vera

Aloe vera balances the scalp’s pH and supports healthy hair growth. Its cooling, hydrating, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties relieve irritation, reduce dandruff, and naturally condition hair, leaving it smooth, manageable, and suitable for all hair types without harsh chemicals.

### L’Oreal

L’Oreal’s cream bath is specially formulated to provide deep nourishment to each hair strand, revitalizing hair from root to tip. Its premium ingredients improve manageability, reduce frizz, and enhance softness. The treatment strengthens the hair shaft, helping hair resist everyday damage. Regular use leaves hair smooth, shiny, and resilient.

### Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Cream Bath session take?
A typical session lasts around 60–75 minutes, depending on the hair length and the chosen treatment.

Can I get a Cream Bath if my hair is colored or treated?
Yes. Our treatments are safe for colored, highlighted, or chemically treated hair.

How do I choose the right Cream Bath ingredient for my hair?

Our therapists can guide you based on your hair type, condition, and what results you want, whether it’s moisture, strength, or shine.

Will my scalp be massaged during the treatment?
Yes. Gentle scalp massage is included in every session to help improve circulation and support healthy hair growth.

Can I combine a Cream Bath with other spa treatments?
Absolutely. Many guests combine it with massages or other hair services for a complete relaxation experience.
