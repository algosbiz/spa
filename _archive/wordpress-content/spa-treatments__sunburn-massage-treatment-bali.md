### Frequently Asked Questions

Is this treatment safe for skin that’s already very red or tender?

It is, as long as the skin is not blistered. Our therapists avoid strong pressure and begin with the areas that feel the safest for you. The technique stays light and adaptive, so the experience remains comfortable from start to finish.

Is the treatment full-body or only for the sunburned area?
It can be tailored either way. If only your shoulders or back are sunburned, we focus there. But gentle Balinese strokes are still applied to surrounding areas to help the whole body relax.

How is this different from a regular body mask?
Body masks are usually thicker and create a sealing layer on the skin. Fresh aloe vera is lighter, quicker to absorb, and ideal for overheated skin. The treatment also includes cool compresses, giving it a completely different feel and effect.

Can I go back into the sun after the treatment?
It’s better to wait. Your skin is still in recovery mode and more vulnerable than usual. Give it at least 24 hours and apply sunscreen before going outside again.

How long does the cooling effect last?
You’ll feel the cooling instantly during the session and for a few hours afterward. The moisture from the aloe continues to work longer, preventing the skin from drying out. For extended relief, our therapists can recommend simple after-care steps at home.
