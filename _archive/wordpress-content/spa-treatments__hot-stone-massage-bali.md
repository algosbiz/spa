### Frequently Asked Questions

Is the heat level adjustable?
Yes. Our therapists always check the temperature and adjust it based on your comfort. If any stone feels too warm, too light, or too strong, we’ll modify it immediately so the experience stays relaxing.

Is Hot Stone Massage good for muscle pain?
Absolutely. The warmth helps soften tight muscles, making it easier to release tension without using heavy pressure. It’s especially helpful for stiff backs, sore shoulders, or tension from travel.

Can I get this treatment if I’m sensitive to heat?
Yes, as long as you let your therapist know. We can use milder warmth, shorter placement times, or lighter stones to ensure the treatment feels soothing rather than intense.

Do the stones stay on my body the whole time?
Not the whole session. The stones are placed at key moments for relaxation and also used as massage tools. Your therapist switches between warm stone strokes and hand techniques as needed.

How should I prepare before the massage?
Just arrive hydrated and comfortable. No special preparation is needed, as the treatment is gentle and safe. If you have any specific concerns, feel free to mention them before the session begins.
