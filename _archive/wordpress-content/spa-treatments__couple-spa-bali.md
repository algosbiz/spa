Each session typically lasts between 60 and 90 minutes, depending on your selected treatment duration and preferences.

Yes. Our therapists adjust the pressure and techniques for each person, ensuring a fully personalized experience for both partners.

While the techniques are similar, the focus is on shared relaxation. Both of you are treated side by side by two therapists, creating a balanced rhythm that helps you unwind together and reconnect.

Definitely. Many couples choose to include a facial or reflexology session for a more complete spa experience. Just let us know your preferences, and we’ll arrange a combination that suits your needs.

Can the Couple Massage be done in our villa?
Yes. Spa Bali Moon offers in-villa services across selected areas. Just let us know your location, and our team will prepare everything needed for a seamless at-home spa experience.
