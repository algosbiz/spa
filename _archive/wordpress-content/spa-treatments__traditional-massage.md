### Frequently Asked Questions

Is Traditional Massage suitable if I’m not used to strong pressure?

Yes, it can still be suitable. We always adjust the pressure based on your comfort, so you can let our therapist know if you prefer it lighter or firmer during the session.

What is the main difference between Traditional Massage and Balinese Massage?

Traditional Massage uses firmer, more focused pressure to work on muscle tension, while Balinese Massage feels gentler and more flowing, focusing more on relaxation.

Can Traditional Massage help with stiff shoulders or back pain?

Yes, this massage is often chosen for areas like the shoulders, back, and legs where tension builds up from daily activities such as sitting or standing for long hours.

Will the massage feel painful?
You may feel strong pressure, but it should not be painful. If anything feels uncomfortable, you can tell our therapist so the pressure can be adjusted right away.

Do I need to tell the therapist about any problem areas before the session?

Yes, we recommend letting our therapist know if there are areas that feel tight, sore, or sensitive, so the massage can be adjusted to suit your body’s needs.
