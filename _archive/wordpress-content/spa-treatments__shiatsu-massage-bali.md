What is Shiatsu massage?
Shiatsu is a Japanese deep tissue massage that uses finger and palm pressure on specific points along the body’s meridian lines. It helps release tension, improve flexibility, and restore balance in both body and mind.

How long does a Shiatsu session usually last?
A typical Shiatsu session at Spa Bali Moon lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. We adjust the duration based on your needs and focus areas to ensure maximum comfort and benefit.

Is Shiatsu suitable for beginners?
Yes, Shiatsu is gentle yet effective, and our therapists adapt the pressure to your comfort level. It’s suitable for first-timers as well as those who regularly receive massages.

Can Shiatsu help with stress and fatigue?
Absolutely. Shiatsu encourages the release of endorphins and improves circulation, helping reduce stress, relieve muscle tension, and leave you feeling lighter and more energized.

Are there any conditions where Shiatsu should be avoided?
Shiatsu is generally safe, but we recommend consulting a doctor if you have certain conditions like fractures, severe osteoporosis, or acute injuries. Our therapists will also adjust techniques to ensure safety for minor health concerns.
