### Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a body scrub?
Most guests enjoy this treatment once a week or once every two weeks. If you’ve been spending a lot of time in the sun or doing outdoor activities, gentle exfoliation can help your skin stay smooth and healthy.

Will the scrub feel rough on my skin?
Not at all. We use fine, natural ingredients that exfoliate without scratching. The pressure stays light and controlled, making the experience cleansing but still comfortable for sensitive areas.

Is this treatment okay if I have dry or flaky skin?
Yes! Body scrubs are especially beneficial for dryness. Exfoliation removes dead skin while the moisturizing finish helps lock in softness. If any area is too sensitive, we simply adjust the pressure.

Do I need to shower before or after the treatment?
Showering beforehand isn’t necessary. After the treatment, warm towels are used to cleanse the skin, so you’ll leave feeling clean and refreshed without needing a full rinse.

Can I get a scrub if I’ve been in the sun all day?
You can, as long as your skin isn’t sunburned or overly tender. For sun-exposed skin, this treatment helps remove buildup and improve texture, just let your therapist know about any sensitive spots.
