### Step-by-Step Nail Care for Smooth Hands and Feet

Our Manicure & Pedicure follows a clear and careful process. We begin by cleaning and shaping the nails, followed by gentle buffing and cuticle care. Each step is done to improve nail condition, smooth the skin, and prepare nails for a clean, lasting finish. The result is hands and feet that look fresh and feel comfortable.

### Essential Areas for Optimal Nail and Skin Health

Extra attention is given to areas that commonly feel dry or rough, such as cuticles, nail beds, heels, and the sides of the feet. These areas are gently treated to remove buildup and soften the skin. Nails are shaped based on your preference, and hydration is applied evenly to support both appearance and nail health.

### Moisturizing and Conditioning Treatments

To complete the session, we apply nourishing oils, creams, or masks to hydrate the skin and support nail strength. The products are worked in with gentle movements to improve moisture and flexibility without leaving a heavy or greasy feeling. This step helps keep cuticles neat and surrounding skin in good condition.

How often should I get a manicure or pedicure?
For healthy nails and soft skin, we recommend a session every 2–3 weeks depending on your nail growth and lifestyle.

Are Manicure & Pedicure treatments suitable for all nail and skin types?

Yes, we adjust filing, cuticle care, and hydration to suit all nail and skin conditions, including dry, brittle, or sensitive areas.

Do I need to prepare my hands and feet before coming?
It’s best to arrive clean, but we also provide gentle soaking and prep so your nails and skin are ready for treatment.

How long does a typical session take?
Most sessions last 45–75 minutes, depending on whether you include additional treatments like exfoliation, massage, or extra hydration.

Can I get extra care for rough cuticles or dry skin?
Yes, our therapists include moisturizing treatments and cuticle care to keep your hands and feet soft, smooth, and comfortable after every session.
