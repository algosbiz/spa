### Ear Candle Techniques and How They Support Ear Hygiene

Our Ear Candle treatment uses a hollow candle with a controlled flame to help remove ear buildup. The gentle warmth softens earwax, allowing it to move naturally into the candle. The process is calm and steady, making it suitable even for first-time guests. Many people feel their ears become clearer, with reduced heaviness around the head after the session.

### Key Areas Addressed in Ear Candle Treatment

While the candle is placed at the ear opening, we also pay attention to the surrounding areas to support comfort. Gentle touch around the jaw, temples, and neck helps release tension and supports airflow. The treatment is done slowly and carefully to keep the ears balanced, comfortable, and well-supported throughout the session.

### Ear Candle Treatment with Optional Light Massage

For extra comfort, the Ear Candle session can include a short massage around the ears, temples, and upper neck. This helps relax nearby muscles and improve circulation, allowing the warmth to work more effectively. The combination supports relief from mild pressure, congestion, or buildup, leaving the head feeling clearer and more comfortable.

Yes, when performed by trained therapists. At Spa Bali Moon, the candle never enters the ear canal, and the entire process is monitored carefully from start to finish.

What results can I expect after the session?
Many guests notice a sense of lightness, improved comfort around the ears, and clearer feelings around the head area.

How long does one session take?
Generally around 20–30 minutes, depending on your comfort and needs.

Can I do Ear Candling if I have a cold or nasal congestion?
Yes, as long as there is no ear infection or strong pain. Some guests feel mild relief as the warmth helps relax the area around the head.
