### Deep Tissue Massage for Strong Pressure

Our Deep Tissue Massage at Spa Bali Moon is suitable for guests who prefer strong and firm pressure. Many local clients choose this treatment because they are comfortable with deeper intensity that focuses on tight muscle areas. This is not a light relaxation massage; we apply steady and controlled pressure to reduce built-up tension while making sure you remain comfortable throughout the session.

### What to Expect During the Session

At the beginning, we ask which areas feel tight and slowly increase the pressure as your body adjusts. The movements are slow and focused, working into deeper layers instead of just the surface. The pressure may feel strong, especially on tense areas, and we stay in communication with you to keep it firm but still comfortable.

### Deep Tissue Massage Techniques We Use

We use sustained manual pressure, longitudinal stripping, cross-fiber work, and targeted trigger-point compression to address tight areas. Forearm and elbow techniques are also used to apply consistent depth when needed. Each technique is performed with proper control and understanding of muscle structure to ensure safe and effective treatment.

### Areas We Commonly Target

This treatment commonly focuses on the back, shoulders, neck, and legs where tension often builds up. We adjust our approach based on how your body responds and work gradually into areas that need more attention. The goal is to reduce stiffness, ease tightness, and help improve overall movement.
