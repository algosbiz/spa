### Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sport Massage only for athletes?
Not at all. Anyone who stays active or experiences muscle fatigue can benefit. It’s especially helpful if you do repetitive movements, strength training, or long hours of standing.

How deep is the pressure during Sport Massage?
We adjust the pressure based on your condition. Some techniques reach deeper layers, but always within your comfort level. Communication during the session is key.

Can I have Sport Massage before a race or event?
Yes. A pre-event session uses lighter, faster movements to activate your muscles. For post-event recovery, we use slower, longer strokes to ease soreness and help your body reset.

How often should I get a Sport Massage?
Regular sessions, once a week or every two weeks, help maintain muscle flexibility and prevent injury, especially during consistent training cycles.

What should I expect to feel afterward?
You might feel light soreness, similar to post-exercise fatigue, but also a sense of looseness and better movement. Staying hydrated afterward supports recovery and helps your body flush out waste naturally.
