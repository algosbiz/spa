### Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a nail art design that suits me?
We guide you through color, style, and pattern options during your session so your nail art matches your personality, outfit, or occasion while keeping your nails healthy.

Can I choose my own colors and design?
Absolutely! We encourage guests to bring ideas, reference images, or just describe what they want. Our nail artists work with you to create a style that reflects your personality while keeping your nails healthy and easy to maintain.

Will nail art damage my natural nails?
Our techniques are gentle and focus on protecting your natural nails. We use high-quality gels and polishes, and we take care not to overwork the nail surface. With proper removal and regular care, your nails remain strong and healthy.

How long will my nail art last?
The longevity depends on the type of nail products used and daily activities. Gel nail art usually lasts 2–3 weeks, while regular polish may last 5–7 days. We provide tips to help your nail art stay beautiful as long as possible.

Can I get nail art on top of an existing manicure or pedicure?

Yes, our nail artists can work on freshly manicured or pedicured nails. We assess the condition of your nails first and may suggest a gentle prep to ensure the design applies smoothly and lasts longer.
